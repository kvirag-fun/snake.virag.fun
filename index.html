Controls & Rules

Controls
  • Arrow keys or swipe to steer.
  • A swipe can start anywhere on the page, not just on the canvas.
  • Double-tap the canvas or press R to restart after a game over.
  • Double-tap or R also resumes play after an announcement, or after a free life saves you - always, on every device. Steering alone never resumes it, so a stray swipe or keypress can't set you moving again by itself.
Rules
  • Eat the apples to grow and score points.
  • Running into a wall or a rotten apple ends the run.
  • Four difficulty levels change how fast the snake moves - Viper is slowest, King Cobra fastest.
  • Each difficulty has its own global top-5 leaderboard, shared by everyone who plays.
  • Make the top 5 and you can add an optional nickname.
  • Whenever the snake is regrowing, no apple appears until it's back to full length.
Fairytales
  • Some say a snake that bites its own tail doesn't die.
  • Beware the serpent-king whose gaze alone can turn you to stone.
  • Legends also speak of a serpent that grows long enough to encircle the world.
